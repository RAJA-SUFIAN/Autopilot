ATTENDEES: Sara Al-Qahtani <sara.alqahtani@example.com>, Ali Hassan <ali.hassan@example.com>, Noura Al-Harbi <noura.alharbi@example.com>, Omar Farooq <omar.farooq@example.com>, Lina Haddad <lina.haddad@example.com>, Khalid Al-Mutairi <khalid.almutairi@example.com>, Fahad Al-Dosari <fahad.aldosari@example.com>

SUMMARY: Kickoff for the Riyadh Smart Parking App, a mobile app that shows real-time parking availability in KAFD and Olaya using IoT bay sensors, with in-app booking and payment. The team aligned on MVP scope, a 12-week plan to a pilot launch in early December, and the core technology stack. Fahad (municipality product owner) confirmed the success metric: cut average search time for a parking spot by 30% in the pilot zones.

DISCUSSION
- Fahad walked through the municipality's goals: less congestion around KAFD towers at peak hours, and better data on bay utilisation for future pricing decisions.
- Omar compared magnetometer and ultrasonic bay sensors. Magnetometers are cheaper and survive summer heat better; ultrasonic sensors are more accurate in covered car parks.
- Lina raised that most drivers will use the app while driving, so the map must be glanceable and voice prompts should be considered post-MVP.
- Ali flagged PDPL (Saudi Personal Data Protection Law) requirements for storing licence plates and payment tokens.
- Agreed MVP scope: live availability map, bay booking (15-minute hold), Mada / Apple Pay payment, Arabic and English UI.

DECISION: MVP covers the KAFD and Olaya pilot zones only (approx. 1,200 bays).
DECISION: Build the mobile app in Flutter (iOS and Android from one codebase).
DECISION: Backend on FastAPI with PostgreSQL/PostGIS, hosted in a Saudi cloud region for data residency.
DECISION: Two-week sprints with a sprint review every other Thursday.
